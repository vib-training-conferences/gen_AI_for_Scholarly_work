# Keyword Strategy: Proteomics-Based Complex Discovery in Perturbed Disease States

## A. Main Keywords

**Biological concepts**
- Protein complexes / macromolecular assemblies
- Protein-protein interactions (PPIs) / interactome
- Complexome / protein complex remodeling
- Perturbation (drug, genetic, stress, stimulus)
- Disease state (cancer, neurodegeneration, infection)
- Differential complex abundance / rewiring

**Experimental and computational methods**
- Co-fractionation mass spectrometry (CF-MS)
- Size-exclusion chromatography (SEC-MS)
- Complexome profiling (BN-PAGE-MS)
- Thermal proteome profiling (TPP), proximity-dependent labeling (BioID, APEX)
- Affinity purification mass spectrometry (AP-MS), cross-linking mass spectrometry (XL-MS)
- Machine learning for complex prediction, clustering, differential co-elution analysis

**Resources, tools, and data sources**
- CORUM, ComplexPortal, BioGRID, STRING, IntAct
- PCprophet, EPIC, CCprofiler, PrInCE
- PRIDE, ProteomeXchange
- Human Protein Atlas, Reactome

## B. Related Terms and Synonyms

| Concept | Alternative Terms |
|---|---|
| Protein complex | macromolecular complex, multiprotein complex, protein assembly, protein module, interactome module |
| Complex discovery | complex identification, complex prediction, complex inference, complex detection, complex mapping |
| Proteomics | mass spectrometry-based proteomics, shotgun proteomics, interaction proteomics, systems proteomics, quantitative proteomics |
| Perturbed state | perturbation, treatment, drug response, knockout, knockdown, stress response, stimulation, dysregulation |
| Disease | pathology, cancer, tumor, neurodegenerative disease, infection, patient-derived samples |
| Dynamic changes | remodeling, rewiring, reorganization, differential interaction, context-dependent interaction, assembly/disassembly |
| CF-MS | co-elution proteomics, protein correlation profiling (PCP), co-fractionation profiling |

| Computational methods | machine learning, deep learning, network clustering, scoring algorithm, statistical framework |
| Label-free vs labeled | SILAC, TMT, DIA, DDA |

---

**Tips for use in Google Scholar:**
- Google Scholar has a query length limit (around 256 characters), so you may need to split String 1 and 2 into two shorter queries.
- Use the "Since 2018" filter, as CF-MS and complexome tools have developed rapidly in recent years.
- Add `review` or `"systematic analysis"` to find overview papers, and use "Cited by" on foundational papers (e.g., PrInCE, CCprofiler, PCprophet) to find newer work.
